What is DashboardAIR?
DashboardAIR is the AI power user command center for iPhone, iPad, and Android. Type a prompt once — DashboardAIR routes it to the best AI tool automatically, renders the answer in-app, and shows the confidence score. One prompt. The right AI. Every time.
What AI tools does DashboardAIR use?
DashboardAIR routes to the best AI for each task — including Claude, ChatGPT, Grok, Perplexity, Gemini, Microsoft Copilot, Wolfram Alpha, Canva, Adobe Firefly, Suno, Runway, Kling AI, ElevenLabs, and GitHub Copilot (14 tools). Free users get access to Perplexity, ChatGPT, and Grok. Pro and Teams unlock all tools.

How do I cancel my subscription?
iOS: Settings → tap your name → Subscriptions → DashboardAIR → Cancel Subscription.

Android: Google Play → tap your profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → DashboardAIR → Cancel.

Your access continues until the end of the current billing period.
How do I request a refund?
iOS: Visit reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in, find DashboardAIR, and select "Request a refund."

Android: Open Google Play → tap your profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → DashboardAIR → Request a refund.
How do I restore my purchase on a new device?
Sign in with the same email you used to subscribe. Your Pro or Teams access will restore automatically. If it doesn't appear, go to Settings in the app and tap "Restore Purchases."
How do I use biometric login?
After signing in, DashboardAIR will offer to enable Face ID, Touch ID, or fingerprint login. You can also enable or disable it anytime in Settings → Security → Biometric Login. Your biometric data never leaves your device.
Why do I need to verify my email?
Email verification is required to access DashboardAIR. After signing up, check your inbox for a verification link. Tap it, return to the app, and tap "I've verified my email." Check your spam folder if you don't see it.
How do I delete my account?
Open DashboardAIR → tap the gear icon → scroll to the bottom → tap Delete Account. This permanently deletes your account and all associated data within 30 days.
How does DashboardAIR decide which AI tool to use?
DashboardAIR uses the AIR Consensus Engine™ — a monthly automated process that polls the world's top AI models (Claude, ChatGPT, Perplexity, Grok, Gemini, and Microsoft Copilot) and asks each one which tool is objectively best for each routing category. Majority rules. No bias, no favoritism — just consensus. Routing assignments update automatically every month as AI technology evolves. Your prompts and personal data are never used in this process.
The app says "routing offline" — what does that mean?
This means the app couldn't reach our AI routing engine. Check your internet connection and try again. If the issue persists, contact us at support@4cylindertechnologies.com.
Is my data private?
Yes. DashboardAIR does not store your prompt content on our servers. Sensitive data on your device is encrypted using platform-native secure storage. We never sell your data. Read our full Privacy Policy.

What happens to my photos when I scan an item?
Scan photos are scanned and analyzed by AI to identify what's in them, then discarded — they are never stored on our servers and never used to train AI models. Only the resulting item record (name, category, estimated value, etc.) is saved to your inventory.
What is Secure Location?
Secure Location lets you lock sensitive properties, areas, or spots behind Face ID / Touch ID / fingerprint authentication. A lock applied to a parent location is automatically inherited by everything inside it, and a lock you set explicitly on a specific location can never be removed by a parent — only by you, directly on that item.
How does Family Sharing work?
Owner-tier subscribers can invite up to 4 family members to share one property. Everyone can view, scan, and update the shared property together. Family members keep their own subscription tier for everything else — sharing only grants access to the shared property, not the Owner plan's other features.
What is the Purge Engine?
The Purge Engine gently flags items that may be worth reconsidering — expired pantry items, clothing you haven't worn in a while, or garage/storage gear gathering dust — so you can sell, donate, or let go of what you don't use. Heirlooms and keepsakes can be marked "Keep Forever" so they're never flagged, and jewelry, electronics, and documents are permanently excluded.
