Questions

Good to know

Is DashboardAIR actually free?
Yes — DashboardAIR is free to download with a generous daily allowance of routed prompts and full access to core categories. Pro and Family tiers exist for people who want unlimited routes and access to every category and AI tool; see the app for current plan details.
How many categories and AI tools does DashboardAIR route across?
20+ categories — News & Current Events, Code & Debugging, Data Analysis, Business Strategy, and more, many built with power users in mind. Six AI tools are fully wired into DashboardAIR's routing and answer engine today — Claude, ChatGPT, Perplexity, Grok, Gemini, and Microsoft Copilot — with more being added over time. The Install AI Tools screen also lists specialist companion apps, like Adobe Firefly, Wolfram Alpha, Suno, and ElevenLabs, you can install for instant, one-tap launches.
What does the confidence score actually mean?
It reflects how confident DashboardAIR's classifier is that it routed your prompt to the right category and AI. Higher scores mean a clearer match; if it's lower, the answer screen offers a "Best fit" suggestion to move your question to a different tool.
Who decides which AI answers which category?
Nobody at 4Cylinder Technologies picks favorites. Every month, six frontier AI models — Claude, ChatGPT, Perplexity, Grok, Gemini, and Microsoft Copilot — independently vote on which AI tool is objectively best for each category. Majority wins, and the routing updates automatically, no app update required. Full methodology is public on the AIR Consensus Engine™ page.
Can I customize how DashboardAIR looks?
Yes — choose from four color themes (Midnight, Ocean, Forest, and Nebula) in Settings > Appearance to match how you like your dashboard to feel.
