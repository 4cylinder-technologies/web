Questions

Good to know

Is AskAIR actually free?
Yes — AskAIR is free to download with a generous daily allowance of routed questions and full access to core categories. Pro and Family tiers exist for people who want unlimited routes and access to every category and AI tool; see the app for current plan details.
How many AI tools does AskAIR actually use?
17 real AI tools sit behind AskAIR's categories — including ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Gemini, Grok, Microsoft Copilot, Wolfram Alpha, and specialist tools for health, creative, and productivity questions. You never have to know which one answered unless you want to.
What if the answer isn't quite right, or I want a different AI's take?
Every answer screen shows exactly which AI responded and offers a one-tap option to move your question to a different tool if you'd rather compare, or just ask again.
Who decides which AI answers which category?
Nobody at 4Cylinder Technologies picks favorites. Every month, six frontier AI models — Claude, ChatGPT, Perplexity, Grok, Gemini, and Microsoft Copilot — independently vote on which AI tool is objectively best for each category. Majority wins, and the routing updates automatically, no app update required. Full methodology is public on the AIR Consensus Engine™ page.
Does AskAIR work with photos, not just typed questions?
Yes — attach a photo from your camera or library and ask a question about it. AskAIR routes photo questions to a vision-capable AI and answers with real specifics, not a generic guess.
