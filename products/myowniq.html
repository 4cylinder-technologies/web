Questions

Good to know

Is MyOwnIQ actually free?
Yes — MyOwnIQ is free to download and use with 1 property, 15 areas, and 10 scans a month, no card required. Personal and Owner tiers exist for more properties, more scans, and features like the full Purge Engine and Family Sharing.
How does the AI actually catalog my stuff?
Point your camera at a space and sweep — MyOwnIQ's AI identifies each item it sees and adds it to that spot automatically. Re-scanning a spot you've already cataloged updates existing items instead of duplicating them.
Is my data private?
Scans are processed to extract what's in them and then discarded — MyOwnIQ isn't storing a photo library of your home, it's storing the inventory that comes out of it.
What are QRIQ© Labels?
Printable physical labels (Avery 6450 round or 6460 rectangular) for your highest-churn shelves — pantry, closets, the fridge. Scan a label and MyOwnIQ jumps straight to that spot.
Is MyOwnIQ available on iOS?
Yes — MyOwnIQ is live on both the App Store and Google Play.
